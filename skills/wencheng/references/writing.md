# Bilingual editing criteria

Keep the intended reader, document type and author's voice. For scientific and
technical prose, natural writing can remain formal and precise.

| Finding | Editing judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated context | Keep background where a new reader needs it; remove a second explanation that adds no information. |
| Rule restatement | Remove conversational promises such as “I will strictly follow your instructions” from document prose. Keep real methodological constraints. |
| Empty emphasis | Prefer the fact itself to “值得注意的是” or “It is important to note that” when the lead-in has no logical role. |
| Evidence assertion | Preserve actual evidence and citations. Remove repeated assurances that the text is evidence-based when they add nothing. |
| Mechanical contrast | Retain meaningful distinctions; replace empty “not X but Y” rhetoric with a direct claim. |
| Translation-like phrasing | Use ordinary collocations in the document's language while preserving technical terms. |
| Repeated conclusion | Keep a useful synthesis; trim a closing paragraph that merely restates preceding sentences. |

## Chinese

- 优先恢复明确的主语和具体动作，减少不承担意义的抽象名词链。
- 保持论文、报告和技术方案的正式程度；不自动加入口语、比喻或个人感受。
- 区分“根据模型假设”这类必要依据与“以上全部有据可依”这类工作声明。
- 保留“可能”“在本条件下”等真实限定，不以语气坚定替代证据。

Before: 值得注意的是，本研究在实验中观察到响应时间有所缩短。
After: 本研究在实验中观察到响应时间缩短。

## English

- Prefer clear verbs and specific referents to stacked nominalizations.
- Preserve the strength of modal verbs: “may” does not become “will”.
- Vary phrasing only when it improves reading; repeated technical terms can be
  necessary for precision.
- Keep field-specific conventions and the user's preferred spelling variant.

Before: It is important to note that the estimate may depend on sample size [4].
After: The estimate may depend on sample size [4].

## Check a suggestion

Compare the original and proposed span for meaning, numbers, units, citation
markers, uncertainty and grammar at both selection boundaries. Keep the original
when a rewrite adds no benefit. A short explanation belongs beside the suggestion,
not inside the document.
